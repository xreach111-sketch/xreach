Deliverable: pages ready to perform
04PHASE 04 // DISTRIBUTION

Daily posting at scale

Your clips go live on every page on a steady schedule, with different hooks and captions tested on different pages.

Deliverable: content live everywhere
05PHASE 05 // INTELLIGENCE

( 12 ) QUESTIONS

Things people ask us

How is this different from paid ads?

Ads stop when your budget stops. Clips stay online and keep bringing viewers, and many pages posting at once gives wide reach for the same effort.

Do you guarantee views?

No one can honestly promise exact views. We test, report clearly every week and put more volume behind what works. Some plans bill only for views delivered.

How long until I see results?

Short-term sprints run 25-30 days. Retainers run 3 months so there is time to test, learn and scale.

Do I need to do anything?

Just share your long-form content and approve the plan. We handle editing, pages, posting and reporting.
